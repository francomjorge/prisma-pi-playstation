# 3D PLAY 1.2

Validation build derived from 3D PLAY 1.1.

- D-pad: thrust on X/depth axes
- Triangle/Cross: lift up/down
- L1/R1: yaw
- Square: movement rate 1..5
- Select: switch UFO/Zurg
- Start: auto-rotation toggle
- Circle: back

Runtime additions: acceleration, inertia/friction, follow camera with lag, infinite scrolling runway/grid and side pylons. Textures and UVs are unchanged from the validated 1.1 build.
